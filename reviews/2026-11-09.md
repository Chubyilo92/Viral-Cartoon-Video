## 1. boy_keeps_the_heating_off_until_youre_cold (a)
**Lines**
1. He won't turn the heating on and you're shivering. Clear. Recognisable thermostat war, but "until you're visibly shivering" reads as cruelty, not stinginess. **FAIL (risk).**
2. He keeps saying "it's not that cold." Clear.
3. A circled energy bill is taped in his cupboard. Clear, and a good visual.
4. He's saving for a ring. Clear. "One degree at a time" works.
5. Send line. Fine.
6. Heating on = he stopped saving. That reads as "he's given up on you", which is a withdrawal threat built on a joke premise. The X/Y slot is filled mechanically. **FAIL.**
7. "Keeping something warmer for later" is a fortune cookie, and it endorses "keeps you cold on purpose". **FAIL.**

**Hook:** Strong recognition: the thermostat war is a proven meme. **Send:** Yes, as a tease ("explain yourself"). **Twist:** No. Line 6 turns a comedy premise into dread. **Risk:** Line 7 praises deliberate discomfort.

**Fixes:**
- L1: "He won't put the heating on until November, and you've started bringing your own blanket to his flat."
- L6: "But if he's saving on the heating and still buying himself a new PlayStation... that's not a ring fund. That's just a cold flat."
- L7: "Because a boy who's saving for forever... will happily make you wear two jumpers to get there."

**Verdict: SHIP WITH FIXES**
