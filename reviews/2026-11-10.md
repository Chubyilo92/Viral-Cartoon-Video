# Review of round 1 drafts (2026-11-10-round1.md): 10 scripts

Result: none ship as written. Four ship with fixes, six are cut, and none beats the current top 5.

## Two problems across all 10 drafts

**1. All 10 are the same kind of script.** Every draft is a single small object or ritual, like hoodie and girl_no_makeup (#3 and #4). None is about a big trait, like fidelity, when_a_boy_loves_you and trust. Those three include the #1 and #2 scripts by IG shares and the two highest saves (31 and 17). The best this round can do is match the #3/#4 scripts.

**2. The turn line is the same in nearly every draft.** Eight of the 10 turns follow one template: "But the day/night/week [the gesture stops]... he/she stopped [caring about you]." Hoodie's turn works because it shows a new picture with a new meaning (the hoodie comes back washed and folded, which means goodbye). Most drafts just say the gesture stopped and call it a red flag. That reads as keeping score and is weak on the second listen. Several of these turns also need the viewer to check a partner's phone, wallet or fridge (see the risk checks).

## 1. boy_warms_your_side_of_the_bed

**Line check**
1. He gets into bed first, and it's not to grab the warm side. FAIL. The bed is cold when he gets in first, so there is no "warm side" to claim. The line contradicts itself.
   Replacement: "When a boy gets into your side of the bed first, he's not stealing it."
2. He lies on your side to warm it before you come. PASS. A clear single picture.
3. He does it even when you go to bed before him. FAIL. If you're already in bed, your side is warm and he can't warm it. A direct contradiction.
   Replacement: "On winter nights he'll lie there on your cold sheets till he hears the bathroom tap turn off."
4. He never mentions it and just moves over when you come in. PASS.
5. If you got into a warm bed, send this to him. PASS.
6. If you ever had to ask, he's stopped putting you first. FAIL as a turn. "Ever had to ask" condemns him for a single night, which is keeping score, and it repeats the beats in negative form.
7. A boy who warms your bed puts your comfort before his own. PASS, but bland. "Thinking about your comfort before his own" is a greeting-card phrase and not quotable.

**Checks**
- Hook: broad subject ("a boy") plus one concrete behaviour. PASS once fixed.
- Beats: lines 2–4 all show the same act. PASS once line 3 is fixed.
- Send: PASS.
- Turn: FAIL.
  Replacement: "But the night you get in and his side is warm and yours is cold... he hasn't forgotten. He's stopped waiting for you."
  This reverses the same object, the way hoodie's washed-and-folded turn does.
- Closer: weak.
  Replacement: "Because a boy who warms your side first... would rather be cold than let you be."
- Risk: low after the turn fix. The original turn ("if you've ever had to ask") teaches viewers to treat one missed gesture as proof he's stopped caring.

**Benchmark:** closest to #3 girl_steals_hoodie (an object ritual with a hidden meaning). As written it FALLS SHORT: two broken lines, a turn that repeats the beats, a generic closer. With the fixes it MATCHES. The cold-sheets reversal is as visual as the washed-and-folded hoodie, and the new closer is about as quotable as "is keeping you close."

**Verdict: SHIP WITH FIXES**
1. "When a boy gets into your side of the bed first, he's not stealing it."
2. (unchanged)
3. "On winter nights he'll lie there on your cold sheets till he hears the bathroom tap turn off."
4. (unchanged)
5. (unchanged)
6. "But the night you get in and his side is warm and yours is cold... he hasn't forgotten. He's stopped waiting for you."
7. "Because a boy who warms your side first... would rather be cold than let you be."
