## Round 1 — raw reviewer answer, verbatim

# Script review: draft batch 2026-11-01

**No script in this batch is clearly better than any of the existing winners.** Nothing is ready to ship as written. The best ones (long-way walk, old jokes, porch light) need fixes and still look like 25-30% shots at 1k views. Two problems run through the whole batch.

**Problem 1: the batch drops the format that works.** The format is: take a behaviour that LOOKS bad and show it's love. All the winners carry that tension: testing, silent treatment, hot and cold, "an option", a scary hoodie, a wedding warning. Every draft here does something else. It takes a behaviour that already looks good (fixing a chair, carrying a bag, keeping a ticket stub) and denies a bad reading nobody would make ("isn't showing off his tools", "isn't being dramatic", "isn't just scared of the dark"). That is close to how the flops worked: "wipes his palms twice", "hangs your photo first", "checks his phone less". Sweet small things with no tension got 0-5 shares. The one exception is the "first message saved" winner (1,180), and the ticket-stub draft is basically a dated clone of it.

**Problem 2: all 10 scripts use the same skeleton.**
- "A [girl/boy] who X isn't Y."
- "She'll / He'll" three times.
- "If… like this… send this to…"
- "But the day she finally…"
- "Because a [girl/boy] who X… is Y."

Line 6 is usually a tautology. "Turns the light off → stopped waiting up" just says the same thing twice. Posting several of these in a row will tire the audience. Separately, 6 of the 10 are "loyal girl" behaviours, and the near-clone about a loyal woman got 126 views.

## 1. girl_leaves_light_on

**Hook:** As written, no. The replacement turns waiting up (which can look controlling) into worry, the same move as the "silent treatment" winner.

**Send:** Moderate. A girl might send it with "this is me". There's nothing for the boy to recognise himself in.

**Twist:** The original line 6 isn't a turn. The replacement turns it on the viewer and works without context.

**Risk:** Line 3 hints at a partner who storms out. That's low risk, but it may draw "why is he leaving after fights" comments.

**Scores:** Reach 5, Engagement 5, Relatability 6. P(1k) 20%, or about 30% with fixes.

**Verdict:** SHIP WITH FIXES (lines 1 and 6).
