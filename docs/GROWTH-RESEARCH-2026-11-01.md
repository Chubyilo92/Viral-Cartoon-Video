# Growth research, 1 Nov 2026 (background — RULES.md wins)

Asked by Chuby: what do 10K / 20K / 100K-view accounts in this niche do that JJ doesn't, and is TikTok doing
better than Instagram? vidIQ was not connected for this run (needs reconnecting), so this is web research plus
our own Metricool data. Re-run with vidIQ outlier data once it is connected.

## TikTok vs Instagram (Metricool, 49 TikToks 5-31 Oct)

- TikTok has a higher floor but a lower ceiling. TikTok views sit in exactly two tiers: ~220-250 or ~680-730.
  No TikTok has ever passed 727. IG is bimodal ~120-150 or ~1,180-1,754.
- The TikTok tier follows the DATE, not the script: 5-6 Oct all ~700; 7-16 Oct all ~230; 17-22 Oct mostly
  ~700; 23-28 Oct all ~230; 30-31 Oct 510-715. The IG winners from 23-25 Oct got ~230 on TikTok. So TikTok
  is an account/distribution problem, not a content signal.
- TikTok engagement is near zero: shares 0-5 per video, comments 0-3, 6 followers total.
- Metricool returns NO TikTok watch time, completion, or traffic source (For You vs Following) for this
  account. That usually means it's a personal TikTok account, not a Business account. Switching to Business
  (free) would unlock those numbers and allow custom covers.

## What the 10K-100K accounts do that we don't (web research; sources in the review file)

1. Shorter videos. We run ~50s and keep viewers 9-15s (~20-30%). Guidance for small accounts: 21-34s,
   aim for 55-70% completion. (Directional data, not rigorous.)
2. The big animated couple accounts (Choco & Pancake ~1M TikTok, couple comics in the millions) are COMEDY
   with voiced characters and a recurring named series, not an earnest narrated monologue. No 100K-scale
   account was found in our exact narrator-plus-moral format.
3. The hook lands in the first ~1.3s as on-screen text. Our first frame is a generic title pill and line 1
   takes 4-5s.
4. Trending sounds on TikTok. We're original-music-only after the 28 Oct mute; that may cap TikTok reach.
5. Possible AI-look penalty (TikTok added a "see less AI content" slider in Nov 2025). Uncertain.
6. Sends matter most for non-follower reach. Our "send this to…" line is right; retention is the weak point.
7. Instagram Trial Reels (shown to non-followers first) are a cheap hook test. Not used yet.
