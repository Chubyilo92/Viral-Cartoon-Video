# JJ puppy dialogue ads — the reusable template

**This is the master template for every puppy video that markets CoupleIn.** It holds what makes a
video a 10/10 (fixed), what changes every time (flexible), and how to read a new situation and adapt.
Code: `pipeline/adkit.py` (builder) + `pipeline/specs/_template_ad.py` (fill-in spec, worked example).
History of how each rule was learned: `docs/VIRAL-BLUEPRINT.md`.

Public link (works from any chat): https://github.com/Chubyilo92/Viral-Cartoon-Video/blob/main/docs/DIALOGUE-AD-TEMPLATE.md

## 1. What makes it a 10 (never change these)

1. **The first line stings at 0.2s.** No intro, no set-up. A line people would gasp at in real life.
2. **Every insult gets an answer just as bad.** Viewers can't pick a side → they argue in the comments.
3. **An open loop from second one** — a label under the captions ("it started over something stupid 👇")
   whose answer is held back until the middle.
4. **A re-hook every ~10–15s** — the walk-out ("wait for it 👀"), the time cut, the stakes, the fail.
5. **Live stakes, on screen** — the relationship is on the line ("If it doesn't help… we break up.") and a
   label stays up until it's settled ("last try 💔").
6. **The app is never named inside the story.** It's "the app". The brand appears only on the end card.
7. **The feature is shown working, step by step, on screen** — its screen stays visible on the right
   while it works, with the current step lit.
8. **It fails once before it works.** The near-relapse proves why the feature matters.
9. **The cause is tiny, specific and relatable** (the last biscuit, a tea made for one, a dinner not in
   the diary). Big fight, small reason = the laugh + "that's us".
10. **The fix is human, not a pitch.** A promise, an apology, a gesture — never a feature speech.
11. **A twist nobody predicts**, ideally a role reversal or a sweet callback to the opening insult.
12. **Then the sting:** a text lands on one pup's phone from someone who threatens the couple → "Babe…
    who was that?" → "…No one." → **full-screen like-goal outro** ("1k / 5k / 10k likes for part 2 👀").

## 2. What killed scores before (never do these)

- Good-on-paper scripts with no surprise ("9 on paper, nowhere near viral").
- A narrator. The pups talk to each other.
- Salesy twists or endings (e.g. "my meeting is WITH Tom" / feature-pitch closers). Keep twists human.
- Naming the app in the dialogue or on in-story screens.
- Repeating the same transition line across videos ("remember why we got the app?" every time).
  **Copy the framework, not the wording.**
- Splitting a punchline across both pups — a twist line belongs to one speaker.
- A like goal shown as a small label. It must be a full-screen outro.
- A text message floating with nobody looking at it. The pup holds the phone and looks down.
- The faaaack sound effect (tried, rejected). Only the soft text-arrival ding.
- Changing parts of a script the user didn't ask to change. Edit only what was asked.

## 3. The beat sheet (flexible shape, ~45–60s)

| Beat | Kind (`adkit`) | Purpose | Flex |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1–5 | `fight` | Nasty hook, both escalate | 3–6 lines. Hit triggers: family, looks, worth, exes, "a break" |
| 6 | `walkout` | Someone storms off, "wait for it 👀" | can merge into the last fight line |
| 7 | `alone` | Time cut + one beat of regret | optional if tight on time |
| 8 | `return` | The other comes back, softer, brings up the app | **reword every video** |
| 9 | `stakes` | Relationship on the line | can be a bet instead: "If it's not there, I'll apologise." |
| 10–15 | `app` / `reveal` / `fail` | Feature on screen, cause revealed, fails once, then works | order can flip (reveal before or after the fail) |
| 16 | `solution` | Concrete human fix with a prop | |
| 17 | `twist` / `hug` | The laugh / sweet callback | |
| end | end card | Brand + one short line matching the feature | |
| sting | sting | Text + "who was that?" / "…No one." + full-screen like goal | sender should tie back to the opening |

Timing: each line 1.5–4s. Aim 45–55s; up to ~62s is acceptable when the sting earns it.

## 4. Reading the situation (how to adapt)

**Start from the real problem, then pick the feature:**

| Problem the couple has | Feature | How it "fails once" | How it works |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fights that loop / not being heard | **Resolve** (one speaks, other repeats back) | wrong repeat-back ("you hate me") | right repeat-back names the feeling |
| Effort nobody sees / feeling unappreciated | **Browny points → gestures** | points read **0** ("you never log anything!") | log what's already done, points climb, spend on a gesture |
| Forgetting important things | **Shared calendar + notifications** | the day is empty ("nothing today") | add it once, partner's phone pings |
| Double-booking / "you never tell me" | **Shared calendar** | it's not in there | add it, see the clash before the fight |
| The same bad habit over and over | **Grow together** (each picks one behaviour for the other) | "how did they do?" — not great | small daily wins, calendar fills up |

**Who's wrong — rotate it.** If it's always him, it reads as a lecture. The best twists flip blame:
she was the one who forgot to add it; he was the one making her tea all along.

**Twist menu** (pick the one the story earns):
- *It was you all along* — the accuser is the one who didn't do it (calendar videos).
- *Hidden kindness* — the "lazy" one was quietly doing it every day (the tea).
- *The prepared fix* — the fix was ready before the fight (the spare packet).
- *The weapon* — the gift was going to be thrown at them ("I made them before the fight…").
- *Sweet callback* — the opening insult comes back as a compliment ("…and you're the perfect height").

**Sting senders** (tie to the opening insult): the ex (if an ex was mentioned), his/her mum ("She's not
the one. Don't marry her." if family was insulted), a best friend, "Unknown", the boss.
**Like goals escalate across a week** (1k → 5k → 10k) so each post raises the stakes.

**End-card line must match the feature, short and plain:**
- Resolve → "Every couple fights over something stupid. This is how we fix ours."
- Browny points → "A relationship full of kindness will overcome the small fights. This is how we turn ours into a kindness machine."
- Calendar (forgetting) → "Love shouldn't depend on one person's memory. Put it in once, and you both get reminded forever."
- Calendar (clash) → "This is what CoupleIn is for."

## 5. Line banks (starting points — always rewrite to fit)

**Hooks (first line):** "I can't stand you anymore!" · "You never satisfy me anyway!" · "This is why I
never wanted to marry a short guy!" · "You never do ANYTHING for me!" · "Maybe I should find someone who
actually tries." · "My mum was right about you."
**Comebacks:** "Good! Because I can't stand your mom!" · "Then go back to your ex!" · "I'm not short.
You're just freakishly tall!" · "You haven't said thank you in a YEAR!" · "At least I HAVE friends!" ·
"Your ex CHEATED on you!"
**Escalations:** "Maybe we need a break." · "You were never good enough for me anyway." · "Then I'm done."
· "At least HE remembered what today was."
**Bringing up the app (vary it):** "Babe, get the app. Let's try again." · "Okay. Let's just check the
app." · "Check the app. If it's not there, I'll apologise." · "Fine. It's in there. Then you're apologising."
**Stakes:** "If it doesn't help… we break up." · "If it's not there, I'll apologise." · "Last try."
**Into the feature:** "Let's follow the instructions on screen. You go first." · "Nice things earn
points. Show me yours." · "Shared calendar… today…"
**Apologies / soft lines:** "…I was so sure I put it in." · "I'm sorry, babe. I shouldn't have called you
short." · "And?" · "…And you're the perfect height."

## 6. Visual, voice and sound spec

- Two pups talk; no narrator. Boy = Kokoro `am_puck` ×1.36 pitch; girl = `af_heart` ×1.24 (`voices/`).
- Captions burned in, tinted by speaker (boy blue, girl pink); mouths move only on the speaker.
- Rooms: fight = red + shake + rainclouds; regret = dark blue; app = calm warm room; end card = pink.
- App screen = unbranded phone (heart + feature name), visible on the right for every app step.
- Text sting: the receiving pup holds the phone and looks down; the message is shown big at the top.
- Sounds: background music bed (user's 3 sounds); a soft two-note ding when a text arrives. No faaaack.
- Outro: full-screen pink card, huge "1k / 5k / 10k", "likes for part 2 👀", ~2.6s.

## 7. Scoring (report every time)

Score each script **and** each finished video out of 10 on: **likes**, **comments**, **shares/sends**,
**downloads/conversion**, **relatability**, then an overall. Overall only counts as 10 if there is a
genuine surprise. Say what stops it from being a 10 and the fix.

## 8. Workflow

1. Pick the problem → feature → cause → who's wrong → twist → sting sender (section 4).
2. Write the beats in a copy of `pipeline/specs/_template_ad.py` (new wording; framework only).
3. Score it (section 7). Rewrite until it earns ≥9.
4. `python3 dialogue.py <slug> preview` → check every frame (phone visible, nothing cut off, props in paws).
5. `python3 dialogue.py <slug>` → QA must pass (no overlaps, audio muxed, <25MB).
6. Host in `JJCutecouple/videos/` (new filename each version to avoid cached URLs), schedule in
   Metricool (JJ brand 7128333), log in `docs/PRODUCTION-LOG.md`.
