## What the 1k+ videos share (and the flops don't)

1. **The hook names a BIG audience, not a personality label.** "A boy", "a girl", "a loyal man",
   "a girl who's been hurt", "you two living together". Half of all viewers are instantly the subject.
   The flops named niche traits ("the planner", "a competitive partner", "someone who forgives easily")
   that most viewers aren't and can't send to anyone.
2. **It's about the viewer's partner, and it's sendable as a message.** The share line reads like a text
   they'd send: "Send this to the girl who deserves it." "Send this to the one who stayed."
3. **A real fear or a real-life stage underneath.** Being cheated on / being an option (#5), being hurt
   before (#4), the messy reality of moving in (#14). Not a quirk.
4. **Concrete, physical, everyday beats a cartoon can show.** Hands you his phone. Saves you the last bite.
   Cabinet doors left open. Towel fights. Falling asleep to the TV. Every beat is one picture.
5. **Short, plain sentences that make sense read cold.** Each line must be understandable by someone who
   only hears that one line. No riddles ("still let you win the one that matters"), no stacked clauses,
   no therapy words ("dysregulated", "rewiring", "audit", "control in a helpful voice").

## Rules for every new script

- **Hook template:** "When a boy / a girl / a man / a woman / [a big shared situation] ..." Allowed big
  situations: been hurt before, loyal, living together, after a fight, long-term (years in), engaged,
  married, new parents, met online, back together. NO personality-trait hooks.
- **Make gender pairs of what works first:** each 1k+ script gets its flipped version before anything new
  (#5 → loyal woman, #4 → hurt boy, #14 → "the first year married", #1/#16 already paired).
- **4 beats max**, each one sentence of ≤14 words plus a ≤10-word "not because... it's because" reason.
- **Read-aloud test:** read every line on its own to someone with no context. If it needs explaining, cut it.
- **Total script ≤110 words** (the winners are short; the drift batches ran 140–165).
- **No line can contradict another** (e.g. "won't say it first" then "says it every day").
- **Share line is a direct send:** "Send this to the [boy/girl/one] who ___."
- **Before writing, re-pull IG reel views (IGRE23) and TikTok views (TKPO07)**; any new script that crosses
  1,000 views joins the table above and becomes a template. Anything under 200 after 72h: don't make
  more of that type.

## Scoring (honest, not inflated)

Score 1–10 on downloads, engagement, conversion, relatability, but **benchmark against the table above**:
a 9 means "I'd bet this beats 1,000 IG views like #4/#5". If it isn't clearly as strong as a winner, it
scores under 9 and gets rewritten or cut. The old batches scored everything 9.0–9.1 while getting
~120 views — that's the inflation to stop.
