## What the 5 actually have in common (read this before every draft — it is the checklist)

1. **Line 1 states the trait AND reframes it in the same breath — it does not hold the reframe back as
   a late twist.** "She's not just cold." / "he hasn't stopped trying." / "Not because you asked.
   Because there's nothing to hide." None of these make the viewer sit with something that looks bad
   for several lines before explaining it. **This directly contradicts the "must look bad first, reframe
   later" rule added to §4a on 1 Nov** — none of the actual top 5 do that, and several recent scripts
   written to that rule (taste-testing cooking, eating crusts, checking a locked door) have scored 4-7
   and mostly been cut or held. Drop the "must look bad first" requirement; a plain, already-warm
   behaviour with a one-clause reason works fine if the behaviour itself is specific and real.
2. **Lines 2-4 are 2-3 small, concrete, physical, drawable beats of the SAME trait, not a chain of
   different claims.** Hands you his phone / mentions you in rooms you're not in / leaves the party
   early. Wears the hoodie away / swears she'll give it back / wears it after a fight. Each beat is one
   picture a cartoon frame could show, and they all illustrate one trait rather than building a plot.
3. **Line 5 is always a direct, nameable send-line**: "send this to the girl who deserves it" / "send
   this to him" / "send this to her" / "send this to the one who stayed." Never vague.
4. **Line 6 is always addressed to the VIEWER'S OWN behaviour or feelings, in second person** — it is
   the only "twist" beat in these scripts, and in 4 of 5 it is pure RULES.md §4a family (d) viewer-turn
   ("if you always feel like you're competing...", "if you ever make a joke...", "if you use her past
   against her...", "if you always have to ask..."). The 5th (`girl_steals_hoodie`) is family (a)
   withdrawal-warning ("the day she gives it back washed and folded..."). **Zero of the 5 use family (c)
   hidden-reason or family (e) mirror** — and those two families are also the ones that have cut most
   often in every review round since 6 Nov (hidden-reason scripts keep contradicting their own premise;
   mirror hooks keep reading as unrecognisable). Weight new drafts toward (d) and (a); treat (c) and (e)
   as a last resort, not an equal-weight quota slot.
5. **Line 7 is a short, standalone, quotable aphorism that restates the whole trait** — it has to work
   completely out of context, as a caption on its own: "a loyal man never makes you feel like an
   option." / "a girl in your hoodie is keeping you close." / "when a hurt girl chooses to trust you,
   it's the bravest thing she'll ever do." If line 7 doesn't read as a strong, shareable one-liner by
   itself, the script isn't done.
6. **The hook names a big, shared audience** ("a boy", "a girl", "a loyal man"), never a niche
   personality label — this finding is unchanged from the 24 Oct version of this file and still holds.

## How this is the floor now

A script is approved only when the reviewer can point to which of the 5 above it matches or beats, and
say specifically why (same specificity of beats, as strong or stronger a line-6 turn, as quotable a line
7). "SHIP WITH FIXES, avg 7.3" with no named comparison is not enough on its own anymore. If the
reviewer can't honestly say "this is as good as `girl_no_makeup`" (or better), it's a hold or a cut,
however it would have scored under the old numeric rubric.

Before every round: re-pull `IGRE02/03/23/21/12/11` and `TKPO02/05/07/10` fresh (the table above will
go stale — a newer script can knock one of these 5 out, same as `girl_steals_hoodie` and `girl_no_makeup`
knocked out `#14 the_first_year_living_together` and `#16 girl_loves_you` from the 24 Oct version of
this table). Keep this table to the current top 5; don't let it drift stale again.

## Still true from the 24 Oct version (process notes, not superseded)

- IG is bimodal: ~1,100-1,800 views or ~115-190, almost nothing between. Script text decides nothing
  about which tier a given post lands in on its own — near-identical premises have landed at both ends
  — so never promise a result, only a probability.
- TikTok views run far flatter than IG (200-730 regardless of content) and are not a useful signal for
  judging a script; don't use TikTok numbers to grade a draft.
- Read-aloud test: every line must be understandable cold, by someone who only hears that one line. No
  riddles, no stacked clauses, no therapy words ("dysregulated", "rewiring", "audit").
- After 72h live: ≥1,000 IG views → the shape is worth repeating. <200 → don't make more of that shape.

## After building: re-read and listen-check (unchanged, still required)

1. Re-read every BODY_LINE cold, one at a time. If a line needs context, contradicts another, or
   uses a riddle/therapy word, rewrite it before rendering.
2. Use `specs/_kit2.py` (`spec()` / `C()`) so captions are generated from the spoken words;
   never hand-write captions that differ from the voice.
3. After `make_video.py`, run `python3 voicecheck.py <slug>` and fix every FLAG.
4. Look at the preview contact sheet AND the sample frames: no clipped pups, no props over faces,
   every prop must match what the line says.
