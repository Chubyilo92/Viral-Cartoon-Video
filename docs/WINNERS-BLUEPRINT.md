# Winners blueprint — the only definition of a JJ script

This file contains nothing but what is actually true of the 5 real highest-performing JJ scripts on
the account. It is the complete template. Nothing is added that isn't demonstrated in the 5 scripts
themselves — no scoring rubric, no taxonomy of "twist types," no inherited rules from past batches.

share is what carries a video to a new person's feed, which is this account's actual growth bottleneck.
All 5 are organic (no #ad), narrated long-format, and still hold up weeks after posting. When a newer

**#1 `fidelity`**

2. He mentions you in rooms you're not even in.
3. He leaves the party early... not because he has to. Because home is where he wants to be.
4. When someone flirts, he gets awkward... not flattered.
5. If he's loyal like this... send this to the girl who deserves it.
6. But if you always feel like you're competing... for his time... his attention... his phone... you're not being dramatic.
7. Because a loyal man never makes you feel like an option.

**#2 `when_a_boy_loves_you`**

2. He'll show it in small ways. He saves you the last bite... even when he's hungry.
3. He might tease you all day. Not to annoy you. It's because your smile is his favourite thing to see.
4. He might act tough... around everyone else.
5. But with you, he's soft. Because you're the one place he doesn't have to be strong.
6. He might go quiet when something's wrong. Not because he's pulling away. He just needs you close... not a hundred questions.

8. But if you always have to ask... if he only shows up when it's easy... maybe he's not the one.
9. Because when a boy truly loves you... you'll never have to wonder.

**#3 `girl_steals_hoodie`**
1. When a girl steals your hoodie, she's not just cold.
2. She wears it when you're away. Because it still smells like you.
3. She'll swear she's giving it back... and you'll never see it again.
4. She wears it the night after a fight, even when she's still mad at you.
5. If your wardrobe is half his hoodies... send this to him.
6. But the day she gives it back washed and folded... she's not being tidy. She's saying goodbye.
7. Because a girl in your hoodie... is keeping you close.

**#4 `girl_no_makeup`**
1. When a girl stops wearing makeup around you, she hasn't stopped trying.
2. She used to fix her hair before she opened the door. Now she answers it in pyjamas with spot cream on her chin.
3. She lets you see her at 7am, before coffee, before anything.
4. She falls asleep next to you with a face mask still on.
5. If she's stopped hiding anything from you... send this to her.
6. But if you ever make a joke about how she looks without it... watch how fast the makeup goes back on.
7. Because a girl who stops dressing up for you... feels at home with you.

**#5 `trust`**
1. When a girl who's been hurt loves you... she'll ask the same question twice. Not because she thinks you're lying. It's because the last one did.
2. She'll go quiet when your plans change. Not to control you. She's just learning that change doesn't mean leaving.
3. She'll say, it's okay, when it isn't... because she's scared of being too much.
4. Be patient with her. She's not testing you... she's healing.
5. If you're the one who's been hurt... send this to the one who stayed.
6. But if you use her past against her... you become one more reason she can't trust.
7. Because when a hurt girl chooses to trust you... it's the bravest thing she'll ever do.

## The template — only what is actually true of all 5, line by line

**Length.** 7 lines in 4 of 5 scripts. `when_a_boy_loves_you` runs 9 by inserting 2 extra beats. Write
7 unless the trait genuinely needs more room.

**Line 1 — the hook.** Names a broad, general subject: "a loyal man," "a boy," "a girl," "a girl who's
been hurt" — never a niche personality label. States one specific, concrete behaviour. 4 of 5 attach
the reason in the same line, as a short "Not because X. Because Y." clause; `when_a_boy_loves_you`
leaves the reason to the beats that follow instead. Either is fine. In every case the behaviour is
simply stated and never held back as something to explain later — there is no case among the 5 of a
line 1 that is left looking bad, confusing, or unexplained for several lines before a twist resolves it.

**The middle — beats.** 2 to 5 short, concrete, physical, one-picture actions (3 is the norm in a
7-line script), all illustrating the SAME single behaviour from different moments. Never a sequence of
different claims, and never a plot. Hands you his phone / mentions you in rooms you're not in / leaves
the party early. Wears the hoodie away / swears she'll give it back / wears it after a fight. Each one
is something a cartoon frame could actually draw.

**The send-line.** 4 of 5 contain an explicit line in the exact shape "If [condition]... send this to
[a specific named person]": "send this to the girl who deserves it," "send this to him," "send this to
her," "send this to the one who stayed." `when_a_boy_loves_you` is the one exception — it has no
send-line at all. Write one; on 4 of 5 real winners it's the literal share mechanic.

**The turn line.** All 5, with no exception, start with the word "But." 4 of 5 continue "if you..." and
address the viewer's own hypothetical behaviour directly, in second person ("if you always feel like
you're competing," "if you ever make a joke," "if you use her past against her," "if you always have to
ask"). The 5th (`girl_steals_hoodie`) instead names a specific future moment in the relationship ("the
day she gives it back washed and folded..."). Either shape is fine; starting with "But" and being a
real consequence rather than a restatement of the beats is not optional — it's 5 for 5.

**The closing line.** All 5, with no exception, start with the word "Because" and compress the entire
trait into one short, standalone sentence that has to work completely out of context, as a caption on
its own: "a loyal man never makes you feel like an option," "a girl in your hoodie is keeping you
close," "when a hurt girl chooses to trust you, it's the bravest thing she'll ever do." This is the
single most consistent structural fact across all 5 scripts.

**Sentence style**, observed directly in the 5, not a separate rule layered on top: short, plain
sentences; no riddles; no therapy language; no abstract thesis lines. "Not because X... because Y" is a
recurring but optional clause shape — it appears in some beats and hooks, never forced into every line.

That is the complete template. A script is approved when a reviewer can point to specific lines and
name which of the 5 above it is as strong as, structurally, line for line — not when it clears a score.

## Before every round

Re-pull fresh Instagram data (`IGRE02/03/23/21/12/11`) for all published scripts and re-rank. A newer
script can knock one of the current 5 out, the same way `girl_steals_hoodie` and `girl_no_makeup`
(posted 31 Oct and 2 Nov) displaced two earlier scripts from this table. Keep the table current; a stale
top 5 is not a real floor.

## After building

1. Re-read every BODY_LINE cold, one at a time. If a line needs context, contradicts another, or reads
   like a riddle, rewrite it before rendering.
2. Use `specs/_kit2.py` (`spec()` / `C()`) so captions are generated from the spoken words; never
   hand-write captions that differ from the voice.
3. After `make_video.py`, run `python3 voicecheck.py <slug>` and fix every FLAG.
4. Look at the preview contact sheet and the sample frames: no clipped pups, no props over faces, every
   prop must match what the line says.
